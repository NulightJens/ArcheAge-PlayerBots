## Receipt and pass boundary

The receipt must bind integration parent, T-081 source/parent, tested module
source/tree, installed module source/tree, host/reference identities, patch
hash, exact commands/counts, warnings/failures, no-runtime state, and retained
unproven boundaries. PASS may unblock T-041 source/runtime preparation only; it
does not prove physical Director refill, one-zone multi-bot activity, scale,
soak, packaging, or release readiness.

client access; roster mutation; scale; soak; release artifacts; global ledger
or lease edits; retained-evidence changes; or AAEmu 3.0.
